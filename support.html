TatLens Support

Frequently Asked Questions

What is free and what needs Pro?

Placing and previewing are free: import your own photo and your tattoo design, move, resize, rotate, mirror, shape it to the curve of your body, fix the edges, and compare two positions on screen. Saving the preview to Photos at full resolution, saving A and B side by side in one image, and removing ads need TatLens Pro.

How do I get Pro?

Tap Save in the editor, or the crown icon at the top of the editor, and choose Lifetime (one-time purchase) or Monthly (auto-renewing subscription). Monthly renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before renewal. Manage or cancel it in your App Store settings.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used for the original purchase.

Where are my photos stored?

On your iPhone only. TatLens never uploads your photos or designs. Imported images stay in the current editing session, and a preview is added to Photos only when you save it with Pro.

Why do I see ads?

The free version shows a small banner. TatLens requests restricted, non-personalized ads and does not ask to track you across other apps. Where required, use Privacy choices on the home screen to revisit consent options. Pro removes ads.
