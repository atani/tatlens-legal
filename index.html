TatLens Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026.

Your photos

TatLens processes the body photos and tattoo designs you select on your iPhone. TatLens does not upload them to a server. Imported images and edit state are kept only for the current editing session. A finished preview is added to Photos only when you choose Save.

Advertising

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. TatLens requests restricted, non-personalized ads and does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Google may still process device, app, network, diagnostic, and approximate-location information to deliver, measure, secure, and prevent fraud in advertising. See How Google uses information from apps.

Purchases

Apple processes TatLens Pro purchases and subscriptions. TatLens receives entitlement status but does not receive payment-card details.

Purchase processing with RevenueCat

Versions that enable RevenueCat use its purchase service for receipt validation, fraud prevention, purchase reliability, and purchase analytics. The currently distributed versions keep this SDK disabled and do not send data to RevenueCat.

When enabled, the SDK automatically sends past and new Apple purchase records, including product and transaction identifiers, purchase dates and status, and renewal, expiry or revocation information where applicable. It also uses a randomly generated anonymous app user ID. RevenueCat receives request information such as app and operating-system versions, locale, currency and network IP address. Purchase records may include purchases from earlier versions and both production and Sandbox transactions.

The app does not add your name, email address, advertising identifiers or a custom cross-app identity to RevenueCat. Automatic device-identifier collection is disabled. App-created content is not sent to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Apple StoreKit continues to determine purchase ownership and eligible Family Sharing access. Existing purchases, prices and free features are unchanged, and this migration does not require a repeat purchase. Uninstalling the app removes local app data but does not delete Apple's purchase history or RevenueCat purchase records. Manage subscriptions through Apple and send privacy or deletion requests privately to akira.taniwaki@gmail.com. Do not post receipts or transaction identifiers in public issues.

Accounts and analytics

TatLens does not require an account and does not operate its own behavioral analytics service. Aggregated App Store and advertising reports may be used to understand downloads, purchases, ad delivery, and app reliability.

Your choices

Where required, Privacy choices in TatLens lets you revisit Google's consent options. Photos can be removed using Apple's Photos app, and subscriptions can be managed in App Store settings.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email akira.taniwaki@gmail.com. For other help, see the support page. TatLens is not directed to children under 13.
